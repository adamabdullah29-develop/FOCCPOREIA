FAQ

Questions fleet owners ask us

Straight answers — including the things we do not do yet.

Is FOCC suitable for a small fleet, not just big companies?

Yes. Starter is built for operators running up to 10 vehicles, and the founding price was set for small fleets — not enterprise budgets. If you have 3 trucks and a spreadsheet that keeps failing you, FOCC fits.

Where is our data stored, and who can see it?

Data lives in a managed PostgreSQL database (Supabase) with point-in-time backups. Isolation is enforced at the database level — every record is tagged to one company, and Row Level Security blocks cross-company reads even if someone edits the page in their browser.

Your team access is controlled per module: you decide which user opens Staff Database, HIRARC, or Finance-style pages.

Can we keep our own branch names, drivers and truck numbers?

Yes — and it is the point. Branch lists come from your Staff Database, and dropdowns in Prime Mover, Trailer, FEG, Safety Equipment and Speeding & Idling follow your own data. Nothing is hardcoded to somebody else's fleet.

Does FOCC send WhatsApp messages automatically?

No — and that is deliberate. FOCC builds the message from your expiry data and prepares the recipient list from your Notification Contacts and WhatsApp Groups. You press send in WhatsApp.

There is no bot sending messages from your number, and every alert is logged in Notification History with a timestamp.

Do we have to move everything out of our spreadsheets first?

No. Import CSV or Excel per register, or key records in directly — whichever is faster for your team. For founding customers we handle the first branch together with you, so you can see the result before committing the whole fleet.

What happens to our data if we stop using FOCC?

You can export every register to CSV or Excel at any time, on your own, without asking us. If you cancel, your account data can be deleted on request. Your data is never held hostage.

Is there a contract or a minimum term?

Monthly billing, cancel anytime. The founding customer price (RM299) is locked for 12 months — after that you keep the same plan at the standard rate or upgrade, your choice.

Information Security Policy

Poreia Technologies · FOCC — Fleet Operations Command Center · Version 1.0, [FILL: date]

1. Purpose and scope

This policy states how Poreia Technologies protects personal data and customer operational records held in FOCC — Fleet Operations Command Center. It applies to all production systems, databases, backups, source code and administrative accounts used to deliver FOCC.

2. Responsibility

  • Security owner — the founder/director of Poreia is accountable for this policy, incident response and vendor oversight.
  • Company administrators — each customer is responsible for its own user accounts, the access granted to each user, and removing users who leave the company.

3. Data classification

ClassExamples
RestrictedStaff IC numbers, licence/GDL, medical and drug-test records, misconduct records, authentication credentials, provider API keys
ConfidentialVehicle, trailer, maintenance, KPI and notification records; customer company data
InternalSupport correspondence, billing details

4. Access control

  • Every user has a unique account; shared logins are not permitted.
  • Access is granted per module by the company administrator through the User Manager.
  • Accounts can be suspended immediately by an administrator; a suspended account cannot log in.
  • User sessions expire after 24 hours and require re-authentication.
  • Temporary passwords issued by an administrator are generated using a cryptographically secure random source and must be changed by the user.
  • Administrative access to production systems (database console, deployment, source control) is limited to authorised personnel, requires unique credentials and multi-factor authentication where the provider supports it.

5. Tenant isolation

  • Every operational record is tagged with a company identifier.
  • Isolation is enforced by Row Level Security at the database level, using the authenticated user’s profile to resolve their company. A user cannot read or write another company’s records, including by modifying requests in the browser.
  • Privileged server keys (service role) are never exposed to the browser; they are used only in server-side functions.

6. Infrastructure and hosting

  • FOCC runs on a managed cloud platform (Supabase) with its own provider-level controls and independent certifications.
  • Data is stored in the region stated in our Privacy Notice.
  • All traffic between the browser and the service is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

7. Backup and recovery

  • Scheduled JSON and Excel backups are produced for customer registers, with generated snapshots available for restore.
  • Database-level backups are maintained by the hosting provider.
  • Restore is tested periodically, at minimum once per year, and after any change to the backup mechanism.
  • Backups are stored with access restricted to authorised personnel.

8. Secure development and change management

  • Changes are tracked, and releases are recorded through the Release Manager module.
  • Customer-reported defects are logged and triaged through the Bug Reports register.
  • New features and fixes are reviewed against tenant isolation before deployment; any change touching company filtering is retested with two separate companies.

9. Logging and monitoring

  • Notification History records every alert prepared or sent, with user, recipient, time and status.
  • Authentication and account changes are recorded by the hosting provider and by the user-management process.

10. Incident response

  1. Detect — identify and record the incident.
  2. Contain — disable affected accounts, rotate keys, or take the affected function offline.
  3. Assess — determine which companies and which categories of data are affected.
  4. Notify — inform affected customers without undue delay, and where required by law notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner and affected individuals.
  5. Review — record the cause and the preventive action taken.

Security reports: security@poreiatech.com

11. Third parties and subprocessors

We use a limited set of subprocessors (cloud hosting and database, email delivery) as listed in our Privacy Notice. Subprocessors are chosen for their security posture, and material changes are notified to customers. Customer data is never sold or shared between customers.

12. Retention and secure disposal

Records are kept only as long as needed for the service and for legal obligations, in line with our Privacy Notice. When records are removed, they are deleted from the production database and expire from backups on the normal rotation.

13. Review

This policy is reviewed at least once a year, and after any material incident or change to our infrastructure.

Poreia Technologies · Notice under section 7, Personal Data Protection Act 2010 · Version 1.0, [FILL: date]

This is the concise statutory notice under section 7 of the PDPA. The full detail of our processing is in the Privacy Policy. Where the two differ, this notice and the Privacy Policy are read together, and the fuller Privacy Policy describes our practices.

1. Notice that data is being collected

Poreia Technologies ([FILL: registered address]) collects and processes personal data in operating FOCC — Fleet Operations Command Center for the company that subscribes to it.

2. What is collected

  • Account and administrator data: company name, branch, administrator name, email, phone number, login and audit records.
  • Records the customer enters: staff name, employee ID, IC number, phone, address, email, designation and branch; driving licence, GDL, medical and drug-test dates; training records; vehicle, trailer and safety equipment details with expiry dates; misconduct and incident records; notification history.

Some of this may be sensitive personal data (health, medical, drug-test, and offence-related records where applicable).

3. Purposes of processing

  • To provide, secure and maintain FOCC;
  • To store and display the customer’s registers to authorised users;
  • To calculate expiry reminders, KPI and analytics;
  • To prepare WhatsApp notification messages, recipient lists and notification history on the customer’s instruction;
  • To provide support, invoice and collect subscription fees;
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

4. Classes of third parties to whom data may be disclosed

  • Cloud hosting and database provider (Supabase) — subprocessor;
  • Email delivery provider — for system and backup mail;
  • Professional advisers and auditors, where required;
  • Authorities, where required by law.

Personal data is never sold, and data belonging to one customer is never disclosed to another.

5. Right of access and correction

You may request access to your personal data, request correction of inaccurate data, request data portability, and withdraw consent. Requests concerning staff records should be made to your employer, who controls that data. You may also write to privacy@poreiatech.com. You may lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Commissioner.

6. Contact for data inquiries

Poreia Technologies · [FILL: registered address] · privacy@poreiatech.com

7. Is supply obligatory or voluntary?

Where the customer requires a record for licensing, safety, employment or operational purposes, providing it may be obligatory. Otherwise it is voluntary. If the data is not provided, FOCC cannot display that record or generate the related expiry alert or notification.

8. Cross-border transfer of personal data

Where. FOCC is hosted on cloud infrastructure located in [FILL: Supabase region, e.g. Singapore / ap-southeast-1]. Personal data entered into FOCC is therefore stored and processed outside Malaysia.

Basis. Transfers outside Malaysia are carried out on the basis that the destination provides a level of protection comparable to the PDPA, and under contractual safeguards with our hosting and email subprocessors covering confidentiality, security, breach notification, assistance with data subject rights, and deletion on termination.

What is transferred. The same personal data described in section 2 above — no additional categories are transferred.

Access. Remote access to production systems is restricted to authorised personnel on a least-privilege basis.

Changes. If we change region or add a subprocessor that processes personal data outside Malaysia, company administrators will be notified before the change takes effect.

Questions or objections. Contact privacy@poreiatech.com. Where a customer objects in writing to a transfer and no alternative can be provided, the customer may terminate the subscription in accordance with the Terms of Service.

1. Notis bahawa data dikumpul

Poreia Technologies ([FILL: alamat berdaftar]) mengumpul dan memproses data peribadi dalam mengendalikan FOCC — Fleet Operations Command Center untuk syarikat yang melanggannya.

2. Apa yang dikumpul

  • Data akaun dan pentadbir: nama syarikat, cawangan, nama pentadbir, emel, nombor telefon, rekod log masuk dan audit.
  • Rekod yang dimasukkan pelanggan: nama pekerja, ID pekerja, nombor KP, telefon, alamat, emel, jawatan dan cawangan; tarikh lesen memandu, GDL, perubatan dan ujian dadah; rekod latihan; maklumat kenderaan, trailer dan peralatan keselamatan dengan tarikh luput; rekod salah laku dan insiden; sejarah notifikasi.

Sebahagian daripadanya mungkin data peribadi sensitif (kesihatan, perubatan, ujian dadah, dan rekod berkaitan kesalahan jika berkenaan).

3. Tujuan pemprosesan

  • Menyediakan, melindungi dan menyelenggara FOCC;
  • Menyimpan dan memaparkan daftar pelanggan kepada pengguna yang dibenarkan;
  • Mengira peringatan tarikh luput, KPI dan analitik;
  • Menyediakan mesej notifikasi WhatsApp, senarai penerima dan sejarah notifikasi atas arahan pelanggan;
  • Menyediakan sokongan serta mengeluarkan dan mengutip bayaran langganan;
  • Mematuhi kewajipan undang-undang, cukai dan perakaunan.

4. Kelas pihak ketiga yang mungkin menerima data

  • Pembekal hosting awan dan pangkalan data (Supabase) — pemproses kecil;
  • Pembekal penghantaran emel — untuk mel sistem dan sandaran;
  • Penasihat profesional dan juruaudit, jika diperlukan;
  • Pihak berkuasa, jika dikehendaki undang-undang.

Data peribadi tidak pernah dijual, dan data satu pelanggan tidak pernah didedahkan kepada pelanggan lain.

5. Hak akses dan pembetulan

Anda boleh meminta akses kepada data peribadi anda, meminta pembetulan data yang tidak tepat, meminta pemindahan data, dan menarik balik persetujuan. Permintaan berkaitan rekod pekerja hendaklah dikemukakan kepada majikan anda kerana mereka mengawal data itu. Anda juga boleh menulis ke privacy@poreiatech.com. Anda berhak membuat aduan kepada Pesuruhjaya Perlindungan Data Peribadi.

6. Hubungan untuk pertanyaan data

Poreia Technologies · [FILL: alamat berdaftar] · privacy@poreiatech.com

7. Adakah pemberian data diwajibkan atau sukarela?

Jika pelanggan memerlukan sesuatu rekod untuk tujuan pelesenan, keselamatan, pekerjaan atau operasi, pemberiannya mungkin diwajibkan. Selain itu ia adalah sukarela. Jika data tidak diberikan, FOCC tidak dapat memaparkan rekod tersebut atau menjana peringatan tarikh luput dan notifikasi berkaitan.

8. Pemindahan data peribadi merentas sempadan

Di mana. FOCC dihoskan pada infrastruktur awan yang terletak di [FILL: region Supabase, cth. Singapura / ap-southeast-1]. Oleh itu data peribadi yang dimasukkan ke dalam FOCC disimpan dan diproses di luar Malaysia.

Asas. Pemindahan ke luar Malaysia dibuat atas dasar negara destinasi memberikan tahap perlindungan yang setara dengan PDPA, dan di bawah jaminan kontrak dengan pemproses kecil hosting dan emel kami yang merangkumi kerahsiaan, keselamatan, notifikasi pelanggaran data, bantuan untuk hak subjek data, dan pemadaman selepas penamatan.

Apa yang dipindahkan. Data peribadi yang sama seperti yang dinyatakan dalam seksyen 2 — tiada kategori tambahan dipindahkan.

Akses. Akses jauh ke sistem pengeluaran terhad kepada kakitangan yang dibenarkan secara least-privilege.

Perubahan. Jika kami menukar region atau menambah pemproses kecil yang memproses data peribadi di luar Malaysia, pentadbir syarikat akan dimaklumkan sebelum perubahan berkuat kuasa.

Pertanyaan atau bantahan. Hubungi privacy@poreiatech.com. Jika pelanggan membantah secara bertulis dan tiada alternatif dapat disediakan, pelanggan boleh menamatkan langganan mengikut Terma Perkhidmatan.
